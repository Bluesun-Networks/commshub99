# Code of Conduct

commshub99 adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Acceptable behavior includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project's users (the first of whom has Parkinson's disease — keep that in mind)

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a GitHub issue marked `[conduct]` or by emailing jon@jonzobrist.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter.

Consequences range from a written warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other community venue associated with commshub99.
